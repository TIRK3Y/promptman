# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

PromptMan is maintained in the open, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space) is expected to make it a respectful, harassment-free place, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **ajaiown10@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a participant, depending on the severity and whether it is repeated.
